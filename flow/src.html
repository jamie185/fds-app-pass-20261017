Same slots as always: email 3 days before, text 2 days before, text the day before, text on the day. 9am to 7pm.

4

The outcome

OK_IBuys the Kickstart

  • App unlocks by itself
  • Notification: "Your app is unlocked. Welcome to your Kickstart, Sam. Open JF Coach to build your program."
  • Opening the app: "Your app is unlocked" screen, then Build my program leads into the Kickstart setup form
  • Welcome email, app-aware version: "I can see you've already set up JF Coach, so you're one step ahead. Your app is now fully unlocked."

GIFT_ISays "no thanks"

  • Deal moved to Not Interested
  • Had the app: full JF Coach, free forever. Gift email "Your JF Coach app is now fully unlocked, for good" plus a notification
  • Never got the app: the normal Not Interested email, as before

WAIT_IUndecided

  • Stays as it is: session tools open, the rest locked
  • This system sends them nothing more
  • Didn't show or cancelled? The app stays locked. If they rebook, the reminders start again for the new time

Nobody unlocks an app by hand. Anything odd goes to Gabrielle.

The rules underneath all of it

Never spammy

  • Every message re-checks the person right before it sends, and stops once they've done the thing
  • The tap-to-confirm notification goes with the text, not on top of it
  • Kickstart: one message a day at most. If a text and an email land on the same day, the text wins
  • A missed day is never made up later

Who's left out

  • Kickstart reminders: refunded, paused, Not Interested, staff and free-challenge sign-ups
  • Kickstart reminders also cover people who paid before it started (from 4 Oct). A step whose day has already passed is skipped, never sent late
  • People who had JF Coach before booking never see locks
