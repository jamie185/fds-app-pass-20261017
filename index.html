Will it lift show rate?

An honest read against the 15 Oct show-rate research. The true current rate is about 68-72% once late-recorded no-shows are counted.

Why it should help

  • It makes the session real. A date, a trainer's face and their own workout in their hand. "Changed mind" and no-reason cancels (over half of all cancels) are what this targets.
  • A reason to turn up. The best parts of the app are visible and locked until the session.
  • Easier to confirm. A push and an in-app button on top of the text link. People who commit in advance show more (signed pre-screen: 86% vs 64-68%).
  • A reminder that wasn't there. App users now get a push about 2 hours before. Prospects had no session push before.
  • Bookings far out. 14+ days out shows at 51%. The app gives them something to hold in between.
  • Selling. They've seen the product, and the trainer can open their session workout to pitch the Kickstart.

What it won't fix

  • Work and illness (44% of cancels). The reschedule flow from 15 Oct handles those.
  • Reach is the unknown. Free accounts used to log in 6-9% of the time with no push. Every message now pushes it, but the real rate is unmeasured.
  • Some people get one extra text. Confirmed bookers without the app now get their later reminder as an app nudge instead of nothing.
  • Longer texts. Most reminders are now 2 SMS segments.

The arithmetic

Lift overall = share who log in x how much more they show. If logging in lifts attendance by 10 points:

15% log in
+1.5 points overall
30% log in
+3 points overall
50% log in
+5 points overall

Expect roughly +1 to +4 points on show rate, plus a likely bigger effect on free session to Kickstart sell-through. People who log in are probably keener anyway, so comparing them to people who don't will overstate it. Read it at least 28 days after the sessions: the share logged in before their session (now tracked per booking), the show rate before vs after go-live, and Kickstart purchases per booking.

What the independent check caught, and what changed

Spring Kickstart setup showed before the passThe V2 rule says every new client owes setup. A locked pass now never gets the setup form; it comes with the Kickstart. Re-recorded: welcome, then the session.
The JFI row in Messages did nothingAny way into JFI (Messages, Training, Home links) now opens the JFI lock.
Welcome video was the Spring filmThe in-app welcome film is now the JF Coach version, everywhere it plays.
Gift email didn't arriveThe test helper couldn't run in that container. Sent the same way the Not Interested job sends it: delivered ("No worries, Morgan. A gift from us").
Could someone claim a booker's login?Checked live: setting a password without the emailed code is refused (403). Pass logins stay unclaimed until the owner proves the inbox.
